About the developments

Viladům Borovského, Byty Borovského and Verde Phase 1 already stand beside Kumpánova zahrada park. Verde Phase 2 will offer studios to three-bedroom apartments. Byty Borovského offers three-bedroom maisonettes with a living gallery.

Visualisations are illustrative.

Visualisations and 3D tours give an impression of the development. The actual finish, materials, colours, fittings and surrounding greenery may differ. Please confirm the specification and what is included with the sales agent.

Construction phases

Five phases of Verde Slaný and neighbouring Byty Borovského.

Phase 1 · Completed in 2024

Verde Phase 1 is complete and its apartments have been sold.

Phase 2 · Under construction

The building will contain 48 apartments, with 67 parking spaces planned in front. The developer plans occupancy approval for Q2 2028.

Phase 3 · Building permit issued

Building SO 04 follows Phase 2. The developer has not yet announced a construction schedule.

Phase 4 · Building permit issued

The separate SO 03 building follows Phase 3. The developer has not yet announced a construction schedule.

Phase 5 · Building permit issued

Building SO 01 has a U-shaped footprint. The developer has not yet announced a construction schedule.

Byty Borovského · For sale

Eight buildings on Karla Havlíčka Borovského street contain garden apartments and maisonettes. Three-bedroom maisonettes with a gallery are offered for sale.

Connections to Prague

The drive from Slaný to the Prague Ring Road via the D7 takes approximately 20 minutes, depending on traffic.

Transport details and planned improvements
D7Under construction

Four lanes around Slaný

Planned opening: 2027

The connecting Knovíz – Slaný-západ, Slaný-západ – Kutrovice and Kutrovice – Panenský Týnec sections are being widened. The Czech Road and Motorway Directorate (ŘSD) expects them to open fully in 2027.

Some new carriageways are already in limited use. Construction continues.

ŘSD sections and schedule ↗
D0Northern section · in preparation

Future D7–D8 connection

Expected construction: 2028–2031

The planned Ruzyně – Suchdol and Suchdol – Březiněves sections are intended to connect the D7 and D8 motorways north of Prague, providing another motorway link between the western and northern parts of the Prague metropolitan area.

These northern sections are still in preparation and are not open to traffic.
